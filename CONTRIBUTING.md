# Contributing

This lesson is developed in the open, and we welcome contributions of all kinds:
typo fixes, clarifications, new exercises, worked examples from your own research
domain, and reports of what did or didn't work when you taught it.

## Ways to contribute

1. **Open an issue** describing a problem or proposal — the best first step for
   anything larger than a typo.
2. **Open a pull request** against the `main` branch. For content changes, edit the
   Markdown files in `episodes/`, `learners/`, `instructors/`, or `profiles/`.
3. **Share teaching experience** — timing data, exercise outcomes, and participant
   questions are enormously valuable for a lesson in pre-alpha.

## What to keep in mind

- The lesson teaches **principles, not one tool**. Tool-specific instructions belong in
  callouts or the setup page, with a tool-agnostic statement of the principle in the
  main text.
- Episodes follow the [Carpentries Workbench][workbench] format: `questions`,
  `objectives`, `challenge`/`solution`, and `keypoints` fenced divs.
- AI tools, pricing, and features change quickly. Prefer stable principles in episode
  bodies and date-sensitive specifics (prices, model names, plan tiers) in callouts or
  the reference page, where they're easier to update.

## Code of Conduct
